## [2.0.0]

- A new app for Windows, macOS and Linux. Drop projects, templates or presets, or a whole folder, and convert several files at once.
- Copies get the same changes After Effects makes when you use **Save a Copy As** for an older version, not just a new version number.
- Before you convert, every file shows what will change and what will be lost, including effects and plug-ins the older version doesn’t have.
- Converts to After Effects 2026, 2025, 2024, 2023 and 2022, also projects from After Effects 2027.
- Originals are never changed. Copies are named like `project_AE2024.aep`; the folder and the name pattern are in Settings.
- Updates install from inside the app, after their signature is checked. There is also a portable Windows version.
- Light, dark and After Effects themes, five accent colors, English and Russian.
- No account, no uploads, no telemetry.
